We built CampusCare for our final-year project: an Android app that turns a campus complaint into a verified, routed and tracked report. Here is how it works. #Flutter #MachineLearning

2/5
The problem: a broken fan or a leaking tap is usually reported by telling someone. Nothing is recorded, urgent problems wait behind minor ones, and students hold back because their name goes with the complaint.

3/5
A report is a photo taken inside the app. The phone's position is checked against the campus boundary, a 63-point outline traced from OpenStreetMap, on the phone and again on the server. No gallery uploads, so no reports from home.

4/5
A TF-IDF and linear SVM classifier sorts each report into 7 categories: 96.1% accuracy on complaints it had never seen. A scoring model sets the priority. Each report reaches its department under a made-up name; the database blocks staff from seeing who sent it.

5/5
