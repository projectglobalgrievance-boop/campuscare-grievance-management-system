CampusCare: Complaint Classification and Prioritisation        [Aug 2026 - Nov 2026]
B.E. major project, team of four | Python, scikit-learn, FastAPI

- Trained a text classifier (TF-IDF features, linear SVM) on 904 labelled complaints across 7 categories; 96.1% accuracy and 0.963 macro F1 on 181 held-out examples.
- Designed an explainable priority score combining category, urgency keywords, sentiment and location into four levels.
- Extended an English-only model to Kannada and Hindi input by mapping known words to English equivalents before classification.
- Served the pipeline as a FastAPI service with duplicate detection and automatic escalation of complaints left open for three days.
