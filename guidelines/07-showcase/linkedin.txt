Project name:

CampusCare: AI-Based Student Grievance Management System

Description:

CampusCare is an Android app for reporting and resolving maintenance problems on a college campus, built as our B.E. major project at Global Academy of Technology.

A report is a photograph taken inside the app. The app checks the phone's position against the campus boundary when the photo is taken, and the server checks it again, so a report cannot be filed from somewhere else. A machine learning model then sorts the report into one of 7 categories, a scoring model sets its priority, and it is routed to the department responsible. Staff see each report under a generated alias: the database rules prevent admin accounts from reading who submitted it.

Results
- 96.1% classification accuracy (macro F1 0.963) on 181 held-out complaints, using TF-IDF and a linear SVM trained on 904 labelled examples
- Geofence on a 63-point campus boundary traced from OpenStreetMap
- Complaints accepted in English, Kannada and Hindi
- 112 automated tests across the app and the service

For our final-year project we built CampusCare, an Android app that turns a campus complaint into a verified, routed and tracked report.

On most campuses a broken fan or a leaking tap is reported by telling someone. Nothing is recorded, urgent problems wait behind minor ones, and students hold back because their name goes with the complaint.

What we built:
- A report is a photo taken in the app, accepted only inside the campus boundary. The position is checked on the phone and again on the server.
- A text classifier sorts each report into one of 7 categories, and a scoring model sets its priority.
- Each report goes to the right department, which sees it under a made-up name. The database itself blocks staff from seeing who reported.
- Students can write in English, Kannada or Hindi, and follow the report until it is resolved.

What we measured: 96.1% accuracy on 181 complaints the model had never seen, and 112 automated tests passing.
